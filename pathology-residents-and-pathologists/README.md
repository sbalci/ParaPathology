# Pathology Residents and Pathologists

Working life in pathology: how residents train and rotate, the diagnostic habits and resources pathologists rely on, reporting practice, career paths, and the profession's associations, webinars, and news. Notes here range from practical (helpful diagnostic web sites, virtual slides, photography) to reflective (the future of pathology, diagnostic reliability, jargon).
