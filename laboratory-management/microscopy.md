# Microscopy

## Cover Glass Optics & Metrology: Glass vs. Polymer Film

The cover glass and mounting media constitute the **front optical element** of the microscope imaging path. Diffraction-limited objectives are engineered specifically for standard #1.5 cover glass ($0.170 \pm 0.010\ \text{mm}$, refractive index $n_e = 1.5255$, Abbe number $V_e \ge 56$):
- [Study on the Performance of Different Cover Glasses in Microscopy](../Clippings/Study%20on%20the%20Performance%20of%20Different%20Cover%20Glasses%20in%20Microscopy.md): Rigorous optical metrology (Jena, Germany; Leica Biosystems 2025/2026) comparing conventional glass coverslips (Leica HistoCore SPECTRA CV) with polymer tape (Sakura Tissue-Tek). Shows that polymer tape exhibits nodular micro-roughness (5–15 nm RMS), high-frequency corrugations (~200 µm periods) that replicate underlying tissue topology, refractive index mismatch ($\Delta n > 0.03$), biaxial birefringence ($\Delta \approx \pi/2$ phase retardation at high NA disrupting polarization microscopy), and causes **5–20% MTF contrast loss** and local resolution degradation ($> 2.0\ \mu\text{m}$) in Zemax whole-slide scanner ray-tracing simulations.

## OpenFlexure Microscope: 3D-Printable Robotic Microscopy

Open-source, 3D-printable robotic digital microscope featuring sub-100 nm positioning precision via monolithic compliant flexure mechanics, RMS DIN finite/infinity optics, Sangaboard motor control, Python REST API, automated whole-slide scanning, and validated applications in parasitology and telepathology — see [OpenFlexure Microscope](../computational-digital-and-mathematical-pathology/openflexure-microscope.md) and [Robotic microscopy for everyone: the OpenFlexure Microscope](../Clippings/Robotic%20microscopy%20for%20everyone%20-%20the%20OpenFlexure%20Microscope.md).
