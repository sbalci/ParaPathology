# Framework

A theory, classification system, or analytical lens for understanding pathology as a practice rather than a specific entity — e.g. the notes under `theories/`, cognitive/perceptual accounts of diagnosis, systems views of the lab, WHO-style classification schemes. Distinct from `Concept` (a single explanatory idea) and `Topic` (a navigation hub). `status` is derived from word count.
