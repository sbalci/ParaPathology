# Concept

Durable explanatory prose about a mechanism, principle, or idea that is *not* a single diagnostic entity — e.g. `what-is-cancer`, `tumor-immunology`, `carcinogenesis`, `chaos-theory-and-uncertainity`. This is the "how/why" layer beneath the cases. Distinct from `Disease` (a nameable entity you sign out) and `Framework` (a theory or classification *system* rather than a single concept). `status` is derived from word count.
